# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing and disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions or restrict participation proportionately to protect the community. Maintainers are held to the same standard. Report concerns through a private maintainer channel; if none is available, request private contact without posting sensitive details. Reports should be handled discreetly, with access limited to people needed to respond. This policy does not promise absolute confidentiality or a response deadline.
