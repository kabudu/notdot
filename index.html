Memory What to remember and how to manage it

Start with a clean slate?

Delete every saved memory, unconfirmed memory, summary, older memory record, reminder and suggestion from this computer. Notes and lists are kept. notdot will restart with a new chat.

Your settings and saved key stay as they are. Old chats remain in Codex / ChatGPT history, but notdot will start a fresh chat. Backups and other copies are not erased.

This cannot be undone. Finish or cancel the current request first.

Notes & lists What you dictate, kept as you said it

Say “Make a shopping list”, “Add oat milk to that list”, “Take a note” or “Read my packing list”. Notes stay on this computer, apart from memories and reminders, and are not sent to Codex. Each person added in People and voices has their own.

Before anyone teaches notdot their voice, whoever uses this unlocked computer can use these notes, as with memories. Once voices are set up, notes need a recognised voice. Voice matching is not a password.

Encrypted backup Save or restore memories and notes

A backup holds the current person’s memories and notes, locked with a password you choose. The owner’s backup also keeps the colour and reminder limits. It never includes keys, sign-ins, saved voices, chat access, or listening and startup choices. notdot can’t recover a lost password.

Use at least 12 characters.

Restore this backup?

Restoring adds to what is here. Nothing is replaced or deleted, and listening, startup and chat permissions stay as they are.

Reminders & check-ins Quiet hours and when notdot may speak

Remembering something does not let notdot interrupt you. You choose which suggested check-ins it can speak. Sending messages or changing files still needs a separate request.

How to use the orb

Click the orb or press Enter or Space once. Speak after the short chime, then pause to send. A second click sends sooner. Escape cancels. Right-click or press Cmd/Ctrl+, for Settings. Drag the orb or the Settings header to move the window.
