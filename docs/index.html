PERSONAL DOESN’T MEAN OPAQUE.

You set the boundaries.

Your name. Your colour. Your voice.

Choose a name, preview your palette and audition voices. Model and effort choices refresh from the runtime’s available catalogue.

Permission to remember ≠ permission to interrupt.

Choose Reactive, Suggestions or Proactive behaviour. Spoken check-ins respect permissions, quiet hours and frequency limits.

Know where your data goes.

Wake detection runs locally. Activated audio goes to your voice provider; transcripts and recalled context go to the model runtime. Keys use your OS keychain.

ElevenLabs is the first supported voice provider. A compatible authenticated Codex runtime is required separately. Service access and charges depend on your accounts. Local memory is not separately encrypted by notdot.
