How notdot works

Speak

Say your companion’s name, or click the orb to start. Pause when you’re done; notdot replies aloud.

Keep talking

Turn on follow-up listening to answer without another click or wake phrase. The orb shows when it’s listening. Say “That’s all” to stop, or let the window close.

Remember useful details

With your permission, notdot can remember preferences, ongoing projects and things you ask it to keep. Review, edit or delete those memories whenever you like.

Settings you control

Name, colour and voice

Choose a name, colour and voice. Ask notdot to change a setting, hear it read back and approve before it saves.

When notdot may speak

Choose who it responds to, with separate chats and memories for trusted people. Anyone can be removed, or remove themselves with a spoken code and a confirmation on screen. Allow reminders and set quiet hours. Voice matching is not authentication.

Where your data goes

Wake listening stays on your computer. After activation, your voice service receives the request and the model receives its text. Optional follow-up listening also sends audio during its listening window. Voice matching runs on your computer: saved voiceprints stay there, encrypted, and are deleted when a person is removed.

Your API keys are stored in your system’s secure key store. Saved memories use your computer’s storage protection.
