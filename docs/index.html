Your name. Your colour. Your voice.

Choose when it speaks.

Keep it quiet until called, or allow reminders and check-ins. Set quiet hours to suit your day.

Know where your data goes.

Wake listening stays on your computer. After activation, your voice service hears the request and the model receives its text.

Needs a Codex account and a voice service; ElevenLabs is supported today. Service fees may apply. Keys use your system keychain. Saved memories rely on your computer’s storage protection.
