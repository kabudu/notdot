# Voice demo · [elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io)

A real conversation with notdot, recorded on 8 November 2026. Say “Hey Ava”, ask your question, then ask a follow-up without repeating the wake phrase. Say “That’s all” to stop.

## Transcript

**You:** Hey Ava.

**You:** Give me one short idea for a five-minute break.

**Ava:** Make a cup of tea and stretch.

**You:** And one that I can do sitting down?

**Ava:** Listen to a favourite song with your eyes closed.

**You:** That’s all.

The exchange plays at its recorded speed, including the waits for replies. Audio levels were balanced and recorded clicks reduced. Only the orb window was captured; the original recordings remain private. The recorder’s temporary microphone and screen/audio permissions were revoked afterwards.
